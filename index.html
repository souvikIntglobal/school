The Learning Pathway

One school, one continuous curriculum — from age 3 to 18.

Children move through four connected stages, each building the habits of mind the next one asks for. No re-orientation, no lost years — just one long, deliberate arc toward the IB Diploma.

Ages 3–6
Early Years
Play-based foundation years focused on language, motor skills, and curiosity — the groundwork for inquiry-led learning.
Ages 6–14 · Grades 1–8
Primary & Middle Years
A Cambridge-aligned foundation across sciences, humanities and the arts, with a second language introduced from Grade 3.
Ages 14–16 · Grades 9–10
Cambridge IGCSE
Students choose from 20+ subjects, sitting Cambridge Assessment International Education examinations recognised in 160+ countries.
Ages 16–18 · Grades 11–12
IB Diploma Programme
Six subject groups, the Extended Essay, TOK and CAS — preparing students for direct entry to universities worldwide.

Moving to Bangalore?

We understand international relocations — because half our families arrive mid-year.

Whether you're coming from the Gulf, Europe, or Southeast Asia, our admissions team has a structured onboarding process designed for families on corporate timelines.

1

Virtual discovery call

30-minute video call with admissions — curriculum mapping, seat availability, and fee structure sent before you land.

2

Assessment & shadow day

Cambridge-aligned placement tests plus a full-day shadow experience so your child meets their class before enrolling.

3

Seamless onboarding

EAL screening, buddy pairing, parent WhatsApp group, and a dedicated relocation liaison for your first term.
