Required Document Dossier

To process your evaluation request smoothly, scanned crisp copies of the following authentic records must be attached directly to the application dashboard.

Identity Documentation

Aadhar Card or valid Passport Information Page (Mandatory verification for global expatriates).

Past Scholastic Analytics

Progress reports or transcripts for the preceding 2 consecutive years (Not required for Nursery entries).

Health & Immunisation History

Comprehensive diagnostic record, blood-group certificate, and official verified vaccine schedule timelines.

Transcripts in languages other than English must be officially certified and translated before submission.
